Nutrition Tips

  • Drink 2 to 3 litres of water daily.
  • Eat protein at every meal (eggs, dal, paneer, chicken, curd).
  • Fill half your plate with vegetables.
  • Choose whole grains like oats, brown rice and millets.
  • Avoid sugary drinks and deep-fried snacks.
  • Eat a balanced meal 1 to 2 hours before workouts.
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours, since poor sleep increases cravings.

General guidance only. Consult a doctor or dietitian for medical conditions.
